# NNNN: Decision title

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decision: proposed
- Implementation: planned
- Validation: none
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none

An accepted decision reads `- Decision: accepted (owner, YYYY-MM-DD, #N)`. Delete this paragraph.

## Context

The problem, the constraints and what the owner asked for. Keep observed facts apart from assumptions.

## Decision

The behaviour and its boundaries. Leave unresolved choices proposed.

## Alternatives

The credible alternatives and why they were not chosen.

## Consequences

Benefits, costs, operational risks and compatibility effects.

## Invariants and verification

What must stay true, and the code, tests, pm-sim scenarios or owner checks that show it. Name the failure that would disprove the design.

## Implementation evidence

None yet. For `partial` or `implemented`, link code, tests or scenarios outside `docs/` and say which obligations they meet. State the remaining gaps for partial work.

Does not prove: what that evidence leaves open, for example behaviour on a real VPN or an installed build.

## Open questions

Unresolved details, or "None."
